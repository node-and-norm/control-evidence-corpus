# Control Evidence Corpus: findings, use and next steps

Reviewed 29 October 2026. This account synthesizes the linked records at source revision `a3a0d9c67997eb958100413ab54d213053d25c1a`. It adds no validation claim, release decision or change to the underlying method.

Status: Development infrastructure. Evidence: Synthetic demonstrations.

## Question and contribution

What can surviving evidence establish about whether an AI control operated, and where must an assessment remain unresolved?

Evidence-linked records, three synthetic demonstrations and reproducible development tools. The empirical pilot and an approved corpus release remain forthcoming.

## Findings to date

The synthetic demonstrations distinguish an issued command, evidence that an action propagated, and evidence about mitigation. Missing records remain distinct from an affirmative finding of control failure.

In the first instruction-comparison pass, each package alone matched the authored reference on 11 of 96 jointly valid judgments, giving no net primary paired advantage for the shorter package. Twenty-four of 120 pairs were unavailable. This descriptive result on exposed synthetic material does not establish equivalence or general accuracy.

## How to use the work

Inspect source provenance and missingness, prepare bounded control questions and test the representation of action, propagation and mitigation.

## Current status and why

CEC remains development infrastructure. Zero independently coded empirical events and no approved corpus release are recorded. The instruction comparison’s decision preserves the packages and disagreements rather than promoting the shorter instructions as an established improvement.

## Limits and next evidence

Synthetic packets, AI-authored references, model agreement and software checks do not supply independent human validity or reliability findings. Comparative pilot roles and thresholds remain unfilled.

Staff and freeze a bounded comparative pilot with sampling, independent initial ratings, adjudication, acceptance criteria and data-rights review before making empirical corpus claims.
